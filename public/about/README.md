### hero

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide photographic hero image for the ABOUT page of ARCH.B, an editorial publication and archive of design, technology, art and visual references. Create ONE original landscape photograph, aspect ratio 21:10. Scene: a quiet, thoughtful editorial archive workspace in soft daylight. A long light-ash wood research table holds an open art monograph, a small considered arrangement of loose architectural photographic prints, ivory archival index cards and a deep forest-green linen folder. In the background, restrained shelves of paper archives and art books and a softly lit plain ivory wall suggest a place for collecting and connecting ideas. An authentic photographic atmosphere with natural paper edges and wood texture, warm neutral tones, charcoal details and one restrained forest-green accent. Wide three-quarter view from slightly above the work table, inviting and calm, no people. Keep the important paper-and-book arrangement grouped in the middle horizontal half so a center crop for mobile still works; background and tabletop provide breathing room at the sides. Low-contrast light, clear tactile materials, editorial photography, subtle photographic grain, natural rather than glossy computer-rendered perfection. No computer, phone, electronic screen, website, interface, screenshot, logo, readable words, headline overlay, watermark, decorative border, split panel or collage. No stone arch, freestanding abstract sculpture, leaning painted canvas, or luxury showroom. This is a single physical archive-room scene, not a mockup of a webpage.
